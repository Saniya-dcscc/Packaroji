{% if page_key=='faq' %}
How do I get a quotation?

Complete the enquiry form with your business details, product interest, estimated quantity and city.

Do you publish prices online?

No. This is a quote-only B2B website. Pricing depends on product, quantity, specifications and customisation.

Can I request samples?

Yes. Choose Sample Kit Request in the enquiry form and tell us which formats you want to review.

Can packaging be custom printed?

Custom branding is available on selected formats. Artwork requirements, minimums and turnaround must be confirmed.

What are your MOQ and lead times?

They vary by format and customisation. Request a quotation to confirm current details.

Do you provide certificates or test reports?

Ask our team about documents applicable to the specific product. Only verified documents should be relied on for compliance decisions.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery coverage and terms are confirmed individually for each enquiry.

Can you source an unlisted product?

Tell us what you need through the enquiry form and we'll discuss sourcing options.
