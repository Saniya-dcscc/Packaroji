Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store in your browser. Packaroji uses cookies or similar browser storage only where needed to provide website functions and manage user sessions.

Current approach: Packaroji's website primarily relies on essential functionality. We do not describe analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies here unless those technologies are actually enabled on the website.

1. Essential cookies and storage

Essential session data may be used to keep you signed in, maintain account security and retain shopping-cart state while you use the website. These technologies are necessary for requested website functions.

2. What cookies can help us do

  • Keep a signed-in user associated with their account during a session.
  • Maintain items in a shopping cart.
  • Support secure account and authentication flows.
  • Remember a user's cookie-consent choice so the banner does not repeatedly appear.

3. Optional tracking

If Packaroji later adds analytics, advertising pixels or other non-essential tracking technologies, this policy will be updated and the website's consent controls will be updated as appropriate before those technologies are used where consent is required.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may cause account, cart or other website functions to stop working correctly.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when the website's technologies change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
